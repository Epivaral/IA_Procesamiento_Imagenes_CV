Understand the difference

Architecture and optimization differ. Equal epochs mean equal passes through data, not equal compute.

01 / VARIATIONAL AUTOENCODER

Learn a compressed world.

The encoder describes an image as a probability distribution. The decoder learns to rebuild it, while KL regularization makes the latent space suitable for sampling.

Image→Encoder→μ, σ→Decoder
02 / DEEP CONVOLUTIONAL GAN

Learn through a rival.

The generator makes images from noise. The discriminator learns to spot generated examples. Alternating their updates teaches the generator to produce more convincing strokes.

Noise→Generator⇄Discriminator

How to interpret the benchmark

  • Feature MMD uses a frozen MNIST CNN, not ImageNet Inception features. It is not standard FID or KID.
  • Coverage counts any predicted class that appears at least once. Entropy measures balance, not correctness.
  • Final metrics use 4,096 samples; epoch charts use 1,024. Small differences are expected.
  • Generation timings use batch 128, three warmups, and ten repetitions, without PNG encoding.

What this experiment does not prove

  • No hyperparameter search or equal-FLOP budget. Architecture sizes are recorded, not matched.
  • Three seeds are a small sample. Local web development overlapped some runs; CPU timings were not collected on an otherwise idle machine.
  • Sharper strokes do not guarantee coherent digits. Inspect all fixed-noise samples.
  • Training time excludes evaluation and checkpoint saving. Evaluation time includes grids and metric computation.
